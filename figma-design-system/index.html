How to Open in Figma

Choose the fastest workflow to bring the design system into your Figma file:

Method 1: Instant Copy & Paste (Fastest)

Click "Copy SVG for Figma" on any screen in this viewer, then open your Figma canvas and press Cmd + V. Figma immediately parses it into native editable vector frames, text layers, and groups!

Method 2: Drag & Drop All Screens

Open Figma in your desktop app or browser, open your Finder/file manager, and drag all 13 SVG files from figma-design-system/screens/ directly into Figma.

Method 3: Tokens Studio for Figma (Design Variables)

Open the Tokens Studio for Figma plugin, choose Load from File, and select figma-design-system/tokens/tokens.json. All color, typography, spacing, and elevation tokens will be imported automatically!

Method 4: html.to.design Figma Plugin

Run the official html.to.design plugin in Figma and enter the local server URL http://localhost:8080/index.html to import the live page with full native Auto Layout frames.
